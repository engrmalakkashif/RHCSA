RHEL 8/9 notes: use DNF and dnf-automatic where automatic updates are needed;
write grub2-mkconfig output to /boot/grub2/grub.cfg on BIOS and UEFI. Avoid
selinux=0, blind temporary-file cleanup, and direct log deletion.
