Objective topics represented: GPT partitions, LVM physical/volume/logical
volumes, UUID/label mounts, non-destructive expansion, and swap.

Safety: inspect disks and LVM membership before modifications. Partition-table
replacement and pvremove/vgremove/lvremove destroy access to data. Validate
fstab with mount -a before reboot.
